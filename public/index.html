Why now

Skill education is now part of every CBSE school year

CBSE has made skill education mandatory in Grades 6–8 with NCERT's Kaushal Bodh books, and a skill subject in Class 9 from 2026–27. Most schools have the books. Few have the labs, kits, trained teachers or a way to run, track and grade it all.

VINC brings the hands-on side under one roof.

  1. Oct 2025Skill-81/2025 Kaushal Bodh skill education is made mandatory in Grades 6–8.
  2. Mar 2026Skill-13/2026 Guidelines for Composite Skill Labs in every school.
  3. 2026–27 · this sessionLive Kaushal Bodh is in the timetable, and Grades 6, 7 and 8 are live on VINC Lab.
  4. Nov 2026Opening The VINC Learning Centre opens its doors in Bengaluru.
  5. Aug 2027Deadline Composite Skill Labs due in all existing CBSE schools.
